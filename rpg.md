# APOCALYPSE ARCHETYPE SYSTEM

## STRUCTURE

4 Paths × 4 Tier 1 Archetypes × 3 Tier 2 Styles × 2 Moral Alignments = 96 Total Archetypes

- Tier 1 = Identity (who you are)
- Tier 2 = Style (how you operate)
- Tier 3 = Alignment (why you operate)
